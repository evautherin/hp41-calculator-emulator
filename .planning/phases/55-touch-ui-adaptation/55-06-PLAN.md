<task type="checkpoint:human-verify" gate="blocking">
  <name>Task 2: On-device checkpoint — hit targets, tap feel, audio (TOUCH-01/03/06)</name>
  <what-built>44pt hit-target overlays (Plan 02), touch-action: manipulation + tap-highlight suppression + pointerdown press feedback (Plan 02), and audio-resume on first gesture (Plan 03) — all live on the installed iPhone SE build.</what-built>
  <how-to-verify>
On the iPhone SE running the installed build:
1. TOUCH-01 — Tap every one of the 44 keys in turn (all 5 rows + ON/USER/PRGM/ALPHA + SHIFT + ENTER). Confirm each tap activates the intended key and NEVER an adjacent neighbor (watch the display / annunciators). Pay special attention to the tightly-spaced digit rows.
2. TOUCH-03 — Tap a digit and watch for an immediate press shrink (scale 0.92). Confirm there is NO grey/blue tap-highlight flash and NO perceptible delay before the press visual. Then tap 5+ keys in rapid succession — every tap must feel equally instant (no 350ms lag on first or subsequent taps).
3. TOUCH-06 — Cold-launch the app fresh, then immediately type `9` and run `TONE` (e.g. XEQ TONE 9, or the TONE key path). Confirm a tone is AUDIBLE on this first use (silent switch OFF). Then toggle the hardware silent switch ON and confirm audio mutes (this is correct HP-41 behavior).
  </how-to-verify>
  <resume-signal>Type "approved" if all three pass, or describe which key/behavior failed (e.g. "row 3 key 4 activates neighbor", "tone silent on first use").</resume-signal>
</task>

<task type="checkpoint:human-verify" gate="blocking">
  <name>Task 3: On-device checkpoint — haptics tiers + error haptic, ALPHA/label entry (TOUCH-04/05/08)</name>
  <what-built>Per-key haptics with tiers (light/medium/heavy) + guarded error haptic (Plan 03), and the AlphaTouchInput bar covering ALPHA register AND modal label prompts with visualViewport keyboard tracking (Plan 04) — live on the iPhone SE build.</what-built>
  <how-to-verify>
On the iPhone SE:
1. TOUCH-05/08 (tiers) — Tap a digit (e.g. `5`) and feel a LIGHT tap. Tap ENTER and feel a MEDIUM (stronger) tap. Tap SHIFT and feel a HEAVY (strongest) tap. Confirm the three intensities are distinguishable (digit < ENTER < SHIFT).
2. TOUCH-08 (error) — Force a DATA ERROR (e.g. `0` `1/x`, or SQRT of a negative: `1` CHS SQRT). Confirm a DISTINCT error haptic (double-tap pattern) fires once. Confirm it does NOT keep buzzing while the error stays on screen.
3. TOUCH-04 (ALPHA) — Press ALPHA to enter ALPHA mode. Confirm the AlphaTouchInput bar appears ABOVE the iOS software keyboard (not hidden behind it). Type a few letters; confirm they commit into the ALPHA register. Press Done; confirm ALPHA mode exits.
4. TOUCH-04 (modal label, D-55.2) — Press XEQ (or GTO/LBL) to open a FUNCTION NAME? / label prompt. Confirm the same bar appears above the keyboard with the prompt as its header. Type a label and press Done; confirm the label is accepted (the modal completes — e.g. XEQ runs / GTO jumps).
  </how-to-verify>
  <resume-signal>Type "approved" if all four pass, or describe the failure (e.g. "ENTER and SHIFT feel identical", "label bar hidden behind keyboard", "error haptic buzzes repeatedly").</resume-signal>
</task>

<task type="checkpoint:human-verify" gate="blocking">
  <name>Task 4: On-device checkpoint — legibility, bottom sheets, collapsible stack, overscroll (TOUCH-07/09/10/11)</name>
  <what-built>Annunciator/stack legibility (no size change, Plan 05), print + PRGM pull-up bottom sheets (Plan 05), collapsible stack panel with chevron (Plan 05), and overscroll suppression (Plan 05 / index.css) — live on the iPhone SE build.</what-built>
  <how-to-verify>
On the iPhone SE:
1. TOUCH-07 — Without zooming, read the SHIFT and ALPHA annunciators and the X/Y/Z/T stack register values. Confirm all are legible at arm's length.
2. TOUCH-09 (print sheet) — Run a program or operation that produces print output (or PRX). Confirm a "PRINT LOG" pull-up sheet appears at the bottom; tap its handle/header to expand it and confirm the print lines scroll inside the sheet; tap again to collapse.
3. TOUCH-09 (PRGM sheet) — Enter PRGM mode. Confirm a "PROGRAM" pull-up sheet appears showing the program steps with the current step highlighted; expand/collapse via tap.
4. TOUCH-10 — Confirm the stack panel starts collapsed (only the X row visible) with a chevron. Tap the chevron to expand to all of X/Y/Z/T/L; tap again to collapse. Confirm the keypad gains vertical room when collapsed.
5. TOUCH-11 — Drag/flick the calculator BODY up and down. Confirm there is NO rubber-band bounce of the whole page. Then scroll INSIDE an expanded bottom sheet and confirm it scrolls smoothly without bouncing the outer body.
  </how-to-verify>
  <resume-signal>Type "approved" if all five pass, or describe the failure (e.g. "stack starts expanded on iOS", "calculator body rubber-bands", "PRGM sheet does not appear").</resume-signal>
</task>
